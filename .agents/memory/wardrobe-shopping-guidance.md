---
name: Wardrobe shopping guidance
description: Keep clothing purchase suggestions grounded in wardrobe gaps and stated outfit needs.
---

When no complete outfit fits, offer a choice to plan a purchase by budget or work from available wardrobe pieces. Until a trusted product catalog is connected, recommend clothing categories and fit criteria only; do not invent products, retailers, stock, links, or prices.

**Why:** Wearwell stores users' wardrobe items and outfit briefs, but has no trusted catalog or pricing source. Fabricated listings or prices would be misleading.

**How to apply:** Use only stated brief details as shopping filters. Budget tiers can shape general spending advice; show exact prices or availability only when backed by a current product source.
